◈ RAJA SIR'S BOOK CONCEPTS ◈

Vedic Math Tricks

Ancient Sutras from Vedic Mathematics, curated and taught by Raja Sir for competitive exam mastery

SUTRA 01
Ekadhikena Purvena · ×11 Trick
47 × 11 = ?
Step: 4 _ 7 → middle = 4+7 = 11
Answer: 4+1 | 1 | 7 = 517
Add adjacent digits and place sum in the middle. Carry if sum > 9. Works for any 2-digit × 11.
SUTRA 02
Nikhilam Sutra · Near Base Method
97 × 98 = ?
Deficits: -3 and -2
Left: 97-2 = 95 | Right: 3×2 = 06
Answer: 9506
For numbers near a base (10, 100, 1000). Subtract from base, multiply the deficits, combine. Incredibly fast!
SUTRA 03
Anurupyena · Squaring Numbers Ending in 5
75² = ?
Left: 7 × (7+1) = 7 × 8 = 56
Right: always 25
Answer: 5625
Any number ending in 5: multiply the first digit(s) by (itself+1), then append 25. Works for 2, 3, 4 digit numbers!
SUTRA 04
Yavadunam · Squaring Near 50
48² = ?
Deficit from 50: -2
Left: 25 - 2 = 23
Right: 2² = 04
Answer: 2304
For squaring numbers near 50. Adjust from 25 by the deviation, then square the deviation. Quick and elegant!
SUTRA 05
Vinculum · Complement Method
Multiply by 9:
456 × 9 = 456 × (10-1)
= 4560 - 456 = 4104
Or: 456 | remove last digit | subtract = 4104
Multiplying by 9, 99, 999: Use (10ⁿ - 1) pattern. Shift digits left then subtract original. Works for any number!
SUTRA 06
Urdhva Tiryak · Vertical & Crosswise
23 × 45 = ?
Right: 3×5 = 15 → write 5, carry 1
Cross: 2×5+3×4 = 22+1 = 23 → write 3, carry 2
Left: 2×4+2 = 10
Answer: 1035
The universal multiplication method! Multiply vertically and crosswise. Works for any size multiplication.
◈ MIND POWER ◈

Memory Techniques

🏰
Mind Palace
Associate numbers and formulas with locations in a familiar place. Walk through your house and recall anything!
🔢
Major System
Convert numbers to consonant sounds, then form words. 1=T, 2=N, 3=M, 4=R, 5=L, 6=Ch, 7=K, 8=F, 9=P, 0=S
📌
Peg System
Link numbers 1-100 to pre-memorized peg words. Create vivid stories connecting pegs to new information.
🧩
Chunking
Break long numbers into meaningful chunks. Phone numbers, π digits, and formulas become easy with practice.
🔄
Spaced Repetition
Review learned material at increasing intervals. The forgetting curve is defeated by reviewing just before you forget.
🎨
Visualization
Make numbers and equations vivid, colorful, and emotional. The stranger the image, the better it sticks in memory.
